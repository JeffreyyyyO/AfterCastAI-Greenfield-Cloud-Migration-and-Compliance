## 1. The Story

AfterCast AI is a forecasting and recruitment company with two data flows. Flow 1 is LuxeMart UK forecasting for 12,000 EU customers. Flow 2 is recruitment for 143 NG applicants and staff, 11 hired and 132 rejected.

The company grew on personal Gmail, Dropbox, WhatsApp, Google Sheets, QNAP, USB drives, and GitHub. The growth created 13 copies of personal data and 73,001 exposure points. The Audit Report records 14 findings, 7 Critical and 7 High.

The Foundation is the story of the move from the Before Architecture to the After Architecture. The Before Architecture is legacy on-prem plus Shadow SaaS. The After Architecture is an AWS Landing Zone in `eu-west-1` with `eu-west-2` as backup. The move is 11 Phases, 56 Sections, 5 Waves, 16 weeks.

This file is The Foundation, the story and narrative. `0.0-prerequisites.md` is the operational checklist. It is a separate file.

### 8.1 Numbering System — Rules

- Phase 0 docs use `0.x`, sequential from `0.0` to `0.18`, with no gaps. `0.0` is prerequisites. `0.1` is Shadow IT Inventory. `0.2` is Audit Report. `0.3` is RoPA. `0.4` is DPIA — HR. `0.5` is DPIA — Profiling. `0.6` is Business Case. `0.7` is this file, Foundational Roadmap. `0.8` is Roadmap. `0.9` is Glossary. `0.10` is Appendix B. `0.11` is Classification. `0.12` is Retention. `0.13` is Compliance Mapping. `0.14` is DPA + TIA. `0.15` is Privacy Notice. `0.16` is Legacy Sanitization Certificate. `0.17` is Appendix G. `0.18` is Appendix H.
- Phase guides use `phase-N`, for example `phase-1-organization-identity.md`. They are not `0.x`, because they belong to Phases 1 to 10, not Phase 0.
- Evidence uses `phase-N`, for example `evidence/sections-after/phase-1/`. It is not `0.x`.
- Terraform uses `phase-N`, for example `terraform/phases/phase-1/`. It is not `0.x`.
- File names keep bare `before` / `after` where they appear: `audit-before`, `sections-after`, `0.18-appendix-h-before-vs-after.md`.
