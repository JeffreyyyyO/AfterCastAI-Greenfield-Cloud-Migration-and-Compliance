## 2. Executive summary

**Recommendation: Approve migration to AWS Landing Zone, AFTER architecture, in 5 waves over 16 weeks, with go / no-go gates. Do NOT continue BEFORE processing.**

AfterCast AI is 3.5 years old, 30 staff, Yaba Lagos + London remote, AI-powered post-purchase forecasting for D2C brands — What will they buy next?

BEFORE has no public cloud. It has small on-prem in-office infra + scattered shadow SaaS. It has 14 findings = 7 Critical + 7 High, 0 Medium. It has 13 copies of personal data, 73,001 exposure points: Flow 1 LuxeMart 12,000 EU in 6 copies = 72,000 points, Flow 2 HR 143 NG in 7 copies = 1,001 points. It has no DPA with LuxeMart, no DPIA, no RoPA, no retention enforcement, no MFA, no KMS, no VPC, no audit logs.

Cost of doing nothing is not zero. It is breach fines, contract loss, and loss of trust. GDPR fines up to 4% of global turnover for Flow 1, NDPA fines up to 2% of annual gross revenue + 10M NGN + mandatory audits for Flow 2, plus LuxeMart contract loss if AfterCast cannot prove DPA + DPIA + TIA. See Audit Sec 8.

Cost of AFTER is small for a 30-person startup. Estimated AWS run cost is $850 to $1,450 per month in steady state, eu-west-1, for Landing Zone + Data Protection + Network + Compute, excluding staff time. See Sec 7 for breakdown. That is less than one breach, less than one lost client, less than one NDPC audit.

Benefit is not just compliance. AFTER gives availability, remote work, and growth: S3 KMS eu-west-1 with 99.99% availability, ECS Fargate + Aurora with no on-prem single point of failure, WorkMail + SSO + MFA for remote work, SFTP mTLS + API Gateway + Cognito + WAF for secure client intake, and evidence in `/evidence/` for every control.

Migration is 16 weeks, 5 waves, with go / no-go gates. No big bang. No data moves until DPA + DPIA + TIA are signed and Landing Zone is proven. See Sec 9 and Sec 10.
