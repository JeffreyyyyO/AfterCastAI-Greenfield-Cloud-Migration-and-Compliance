**THIS APPENDIX CONTAINS SIMULATED EVIDENCE ONLY.**

They do not come from a real Gmail account, a real Dropbox account, a real QNAP NAS, a real GitHub repo, a real Google Sheet, or a real USB drive. No real personal data was used. AfterCast AI is fictional. LuxeMart UK is fictional. It is convincing as a demonstration of audit method, not as proof of a real breach.

**Reading each item:** Each item has: Evidence ID, Finding link, What real evidence would show, Simulated mock, Interpretation, Control failure, After Architecture fix. The mock is labeled SIMULATED on every item. This pack is presented to a regulator as simulated evidence only, never as real evidence.
