## 3. What Data Is Collected — And When

### 3.1 At Application — CV Only

At application, only the data needed to screen an application is collected:

| Data | Category | Needed to Apply? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Name | PII | Yes, to contact you |
| Email | PII | Yes, to contact you |
| Phone | PII | Yes, to contact you |
| CV, Work history, Education | PII | Yes, to screen |

No other data is collected at application. No NIN, no BVN, no Passport, no Bank Details, no Home Address are collected at application.

### 3.2 After Conditional Offer — Sensitive Only

After a conditional offer, and only after a conditional offer, the following are collected, via secure portal, with explicit consent checkbox:

| Data | Category | Needed After Offer? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Home Address | PII | Yes, after offer, for HR file |
| Passport scan | Sensitive, Identity Document | Yes, after offer, to verify identity |
| NIN | Sensitive, National ID | Yes, after offer, where lawful and necessary for employment |
| BVN | Sensitive, Financial ID | Yes, after offer, to set up bank payment, only where bank requires it |
| Bank Details | Sensitive, Financial | Yes, after offer, for payroll |

NIN and BVN are not needed to screen CVs. They are not needed to decide who to interview. They are needed only after a conditional offer, to verify identity, run background checks where lawful, and set up payroll and bank payment. Collecting them at application stage is excessive and violates minimisation.

**DataClass:** At application, data is Restricted-PII. After offer, data is Restricted-PII + Sensitive. See `0.11-data-classification-policy.md`, Sec 4.5.

## 5. Lawful Basis

| Processing | GDPR | NDPA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Screening CVs, contacting you | Art 6(1)(b) Contract, pre-contractual steps at your request. Art 6(1)(f) Legitimate Interests for recruitment, where applicable. | Sec 25 Contract, pre-contractual. Sec 25 Legitimate Interests for recruitment. |
| Collecting NIN, BVN, Passport, Bank Details after offer | Art 6(1)(b) Contract, Art 6(1)(c) Legal Obligation for employment / tax, plus Art 9(2)(b) Employment law for Sensitive, where applicable, plus explicit consent | Sec 25 Contract, Sec 25 Legal Obligation, plus Sec 35 explicit consent for Sensitive, plus Sec 44 DPIA |
| Storing hired data 6 years | Art 6(1)(c) Legal Obligation, employment / tax retention | Sec 25 Legal Obligation, Sec 24(d) Storage Limitation, 6y |
| Deleting rejected data 180 days | Art 5(1)(e) Storage Limitation | Sec 24(d) Storage Limitation |

**Consent and power imbalance:** Consent alone is not sufficient for Sensitive data where there is power imbalance. Applicants cannot freely consent where the Form requires NIN / BVN to apply. The After Architecture uses Contract + Legal Obligation + explicit consent + DPIA, not consent alone. The Before Architecture had no documented lawful basis and no consent record.

**Whether provision is required:** Name, Email, Phone, CV are required to consider your application. Where you choose not to provide them, AfterCast AI is unable to consider your application. NIN, BVN, Passport, Bank Details, Home Address are required only after conditional offer, where lawful and necessary. Where you choose not to provide them after offer, AfterCast AI is unable to complete onboarding or payroll.

**Automated decision-making:** No solely automated decision with legal / similarly significant effect is made on your application. Screening is by HR, with human review. No profiling for hiring is done by AI model. Flow 1 profiling in `0.5-dpia-luxemart-profiling.md` is for LuxeMart customers, not for applicants.

## 9. Your Rights

You have the following rights, under GDPR Art 15 to Art 21, and NDPA Sec 35 to Sec 36:

| Right | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| Access | You can ask what data AfterCast AI holds about you, and receive a copy. |
| Rectification | You can ask for inaccurate data to be corrected. |
| Erasure | You can ask for your data to be deleted, where the conditions for erasure are met. Deletion deletes all copies: S3, Aurora, Backup, Logs, with proof. |
| Restriction | You can ask for processing to be restricted, where the conditions for restriction are met. |
| Portability | You can ask for your data in a structured, commonly used format, where the conditions for portability are met. |
| Objection | You can object to processing based on Legitimate Interests, where the conditions for objection are met. |
| Withdrawal of consent | You can withdraw consent for Sensitive data, where consent is the basis. Withdrawal does not affect lawfulness before withdrawal. Withdrawal is via DSR API, NDPA Sec 35, GDPR Art 7. |

**Exercising your rights:** Contact DPO Kenneth Pratt, compliance@aftercast.ai, or submit via DSR API `POST /dsr`. AfterCast AI responds within the statutory time, and confirms erasure with proof, where erasure applies. For Flow 2, DSR is via AfterCast AI, with deletion + proof. For Flow 1, DSR is via LuxeMart, with hashed ID + retraining — Flow 1 is not covered by this notice.

**No fee:** Exercising your rights is free, except where the request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, as allowed by law.
