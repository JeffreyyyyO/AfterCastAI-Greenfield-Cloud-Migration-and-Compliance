## 2. Executive Summary

Decision: High risk in the Before Architecture. High risk is reduced to Medium residual risk in the After Architecture, where all After Architecture controls in Sec 8 are implemented. Processing in the Before Architecture form is high risk. Training a model on raw emails is high risk.

The Before Architecture collected LuxeMart CSV via WeTransfer, stored in Funmi Daniels personal Gmail, Dropbox anyone-with-link US, WhatsApp group with ex-staff, Amara Eze MacBook, local Postgres, and public GitHub `model.pkl` with raw emails. Copies: 6. Exposure: 72,000 points. Findings: F1-01 to F1-07, 3 Critical + 4 High.

The most severe risks are not only breach. They are profiling harm and model memorisation:

- Profiling: AfterCast predicts what a person will buy next. That profile can be used to target, nudge, price, or exclude. Where the profile is wrong, the person is misclassified. Where the profile leaks, the person is exposed.
- Model memorisation: `model.pkl` trained on raw emails memorises emails. The model file itself becomes PII. Deleting the CSV does not delete the model. Anyone with the model can extract emails.
- Re-identification: Even where name is removed, email + purchase history re-identifies. Pseudonymisation is done before training, not after.

The After Architecture fixes this by: SFTP mTLS intake, Lambda validate + classify + pseudonymise email to `sha256_hashed_id` before any training, Lake Formation sales-group only, VPC Endpoint PrivateLink, ECS Fargate STS temp creds 1h, Aurora with hashed ID only, Model Registry with hashed ID only, raw CSV deleted after 365 days, logs kept 6y in Glacier Deep Archive with PII not kept 6y, DSR erasure via hashed ID + retraining.

Residual risk remains Medium, because profiling is inherently high risk, even with pseudonymisation. Residual risk is acceptable with DPO oversight, model governance, quarterly review, and LuxeMart DPA + TIA in place.

## 3. Description of Processing

### 3.1 Purpose

Predict what a LuxeMart customer will buy next, to improve forecasting, stock, and marketing. Purpose is limited to forecasting as instructed by LuxeMart in DPA. AfterCast AI uses LuxeMart data for no own purposes, for no other clients, for no advertising, and for no model improvement outside the DPA.

### 3.2 What Is Not the Purpose

- AfterCast AI profiles for no credit, insurance, employment, health, or any decision with legal or similarly significant effect, unless LuxeMart explicitly instructs it and documents a separate DPIA and Art 22 assessment.
- AfterCast AI sells, shares, or re-uses LuxeMart data in no part.
- AfterCast AI trains no shared foundation model on LuxeMart data and then uses that model for other clients, unless LuxeMart explicitly authorises it and pseudonymisation + separation are proven.

### 3.3 Data Subjects

LuxeMart UK customers, 12,000 EU data subjects.

- Location: EU, primarily UK, per Audit Sec 4.
- DataClass: Restricted-PII, EU.
- Vulnerability: Customers did not directly give data to AfterCast AI. They gave it to LuxeMart. They may not know AfterCast AI exists. Transparency via LuxeMart privacy notice is essential.

### 3.4 Personal Data

| Data | Category | Used for Training? | After Architecture Treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Name | PII | No | Pseudonymised, not used for training, stored in S3 KMS only where needed for DSR mapping, separate table |
| Email | PII, direct identifier | No, raw email is not used for training | Hashed to `sha256_hashed_id` with salt, before training. Raw email in S3 KMS only, not in Aurora training table, not in model. |

## 4. Lawful Basis, Necessity, Proportionality

### 4.1 Lawful Basis

| Processing | GDPR | NDPA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| LuxeMart collecting customer data for forecasting | Art 6(1)(b) Contract with customer, or Art 6(1)(f) Legitimate Interests, as documented by LuxeMart in its own DPIA and privacy notice. AfterCast AI does not decide this basis. | Sec 25 Contract or Legitimate Interests, as documented by LuxeMart. |
| AfterCast AI processing on behalf of LuxeMart | Art 6(1)(b) Contract via DPA, Art 28 Processor. AfterCast AI acts only on documented instructions from LuxeMart. | Sec 25 Contract via DPA, Sec 29 Processor. AfterCast AI acts only on LuxeMart instructions. |
| Profiling, forecasting what a person will buy next | Art 6 + Art 22, where profiling produces legal or similarly significant effect. Assessment is by LuxeMart. AfterCast AI flags Art 22 risk to LuxeMart. | Sec 25 + Sec 44 DPIA, plus Sec 27 Transparency. Profiling is disclosed in LuxeMart privacy notice. |
| Pseudonymisation, security | Art 5(1)(f) + Art 32, Art 25 By Design | Sec 24 + Sec 39, Sec 44 |
| Transfers to AfterCast AI in Nigeria, where any | Art 44 + Art 46, SCCs + TIA + DPA, see Sec 6 | Sec 41 Cross-Border, adequate safeguards + DPA |

**What is not a lawful basis:** Legitimate Interest + Consent alone is not enough for AfterCast AI as processor. AfterCast AI needs a DPA with LuxeMart, with documented instructions, plus LuxeMart own lawful basis with customers. The Before Architecture has no DPA, no SCCs, no documented instructions. The After Architecture includes a DPA before any new intake.

### 4.2 Necessity Test

| Question | Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| Is raw email needed to forecast? | No. Hashed ID is enough to join purchases over time. |
| Is name needed to forecast? | No. Name is not a forecasting feature. |
| Is phone needed to forecast? | No. Phone is not a forecasting feature. |
| Is full delivery address needed? | No. Region or postcode district is enough, where location is needed at all. |
| Is full purchase history needed? | Yes, but only as pseudonymised features, with minimisation: last 12 months, or as instructed by LuxeMart, not infinite history. |
| Could the purpose be achieved with less data? | Yes. Pseudonymise before training, minimise features, delete raw CSV after 365 days. That is the After Architecture design. |

**Proportionality conclusion:** The Before Architecture was disproportionate. It collected raw identifiers, kept them forever, in 6 copies, and trained on raw emails. The After Architecture is proportionate. It pseudonymises before training, keeps raw CSV only 365 days, single copy, and trains only on hashed features.

### 4.3 Automated Decision-Making, Art 22

AfterCast AI forecasting is profiling. Where LuxeMart uses the forecast to make a decision that produces legal or similarly significant effect, for example denying a discount, excluding from an offer, or setting a price, then GDPR Art 22 applies.

AfterCast AI actions in the DPA:

- Ask LuxeMart in DPA: Will forecast be used for Art 22 decisions? Where the answer is yes, safeguards, human review, right to object, and right to contest apply.
- Log model version, features, and `hashed_id` for each forecast, so a decision can be explained and contested.
- No claim that the model is fully automated and unchallengeable. Human review path is via LuxeMart.
- Where LuxeMart says no Art 22 decisions, that is documented in DPA and re-assessed where purpose changes.

The Before Architecture has no Art 22 assessment. The After Architecture includes an Art 22 assessment in DPA before intake.

### 4.4 Transparency

Before Architecture: Customers did not know AfterCast AI existed. No transparency.

After Architecture: LuxeMart updates its privacy notice to name AfterCast AI as processor, state purpose forecasting, state profiling, state retention 365 days, state rights, state transfers to Nigeria where any, with SCCs + TIA summary. AfterCast AI provides LuxeMart with processor transparency text, to be included in LuxeMart notice.

## 9. Pseudonymisation and DSR Erasure — Technical Detail

This section is specific to profiling DPIA. It is implemented in Terraform + Lambda + Model Registry, not only in policy.

### 9.1 Pseudonymisation Design

- **Input:** Raw CSV in S3, `s3://aftercast-prod-restricted-pii-eu-west-1/raw/`, KMS CMK, `eu-west-1`, Block Public, Versioning.
- **Lambda:** `lambda-pseudonymise`, triggered by S3 Event, validates, classifies, pseudonymises.
- **Hash:** Email to `sha256_hashed_id` = `SHA256(email_lower_trimmed + salt)`. Salt stored in Secrets Manager, KMS, not in code, not in model, not in Aurora training table.
- **Mapping:** Mapping table `email -> hashed_id` stored in separate S3 prefix `mapping/`, KMS, Lake Formation sales-group + DPO only, not Data Science. Data Science never sees raw email.
- **Training table:** Aurora `aftercast-prod-aurora-eu-west-1`, table `forecasting_features`, columns `hashed_id, purchase_features`, no name, no email, no phone, no address. `SELECT hashed_id, purchase_features` only.
- **Model:** Model Registry, `hashed_id` only, no raw email, no `model.pkl` with raw emails, versioned, with feature list, training date, and DSR retraining log.
- **Logs:** CloudTrail + S3 Inventory, no PII in logs, or pseudonymised hashed ID only. Where a log contains raw email, it is redacted before it is kept 6y.

### 9.2 DSR Erasure via Hashed ID

Right to Erasure, GDPR Art 17, NDPA Sec 36, is hard for AI models. Deleting a row does not delete model memory. The After Architecture does:

1. **Request:** Data subject contacts LuxeMart, LuxeMart forwards DSR to AfterCast AI via DPA, with `hashed_id` or email to be hashed via same salt, to find `hashed_id`.
2. **Delete raw:** Delete raw CSV row from S3, delete mapping row from mapping table, delete Aurora row for `hashed_id`.
3. **Delete model version:** Mark model version that was trained on that `hashed_id` as tainted, not used for new forecasts.
4. **Retrain:** Retrain model without that `hashed_id`, create new model version, with retraining log, date, and DSR ID.
5. **Proof:** S3 Inventory + CloudTrail DeleteObject + Macie + Model Registry version log, kept 6y in Glacier Deep Archive, with PII not kept 6y, proof contains `hashed_id` + DSR ID only, not raw email.
6. **Tell LuxeMart:** Confirm erasure to LuxeMart within DPA SLA, so LuxeMart can confirm to data subject.

**What is not erasure:** Deleting the CSV but keeping the model is not erasure. Keeping `model.pkl` with raw emails is not erasure. Keeping the mapping table is not erasure.

### 9.3 Model Governance

- Model versioning: Every model version has version, training date, feature list, hashed ID count, DSR retraining log, and approval by DPO + Data Science.
- Memorisation test: Before deployment, test model for memorisation of training data, for example membership inference test, and document result. Where memorisation is high, retrain with regularisation, differential privacy, or fewer epochs, as documented in model card.
- Bias test: Before deployment, test for bias by region, gender, or other protected characteristic, where LuxeMart instructs it, and document result. Where bias is found, deployment is with LuxeMart approval and mitigation.
- Model card: Each model version has a model card, with purpose, features, limitations, Art 22 assessment, and DSR retraining log.

### 13.1 Numbering System — Rules

- Phase 0 docs use `0.x`, sequential from `0.0` to `0.18`, with no gaps. `0.0` is prerequisites. `0.1` is Shadow IT Inventory. `0.2` is Audit Report. `0.3` is RoPA. `0.4` is DPIA — HR. `0.5` is this file, DPIA — Profiling. `0.6` is Business Case. `0.7` is Foundational Roadmap. `0.8` is Roadmap. `0.9` is Glossary. `0.10` is Appendix B. `0.11` is Classification. `0.12` is Retention. `0.13` is Compliance Mapping. `0.14` is DPA + TIA. `0.15` is Privacy Notice. `0.16` is Legacy Sanitization Certificate. `0.17` is Appendix G. `0.18` is Appendix H.
- Phase guides use `phase-N`, for example `phase-1-organization-identity.md`. They are not `0.x`, because they belong to Phases 1 to 10, not Phase 0.
- Evidence uses `phase-N`, for example `evidence/sections-after/phase-1/`. It is not `0.x`.
- Terraform uses `phase-N`, for example `terraform/phases/phase-1/`. It is not `0.x`.
- File names keep bare `before` / `after` where they appear: `audit-before`, `sections-after`, `0.18-appendix-h-before-vs-after.md`.

## 14. Rules — 1 Fact, 1 Name

- **1 Fact, 1 Name:** Character, Location, Count, DataClass match exactly across Inventory, Diagram, Findings, RoPA, DPIA, Tables, Appendix B, Roadmap. Auditor Ctrl+F finds the same name everywhere.
- **One fact, one file:** Glossary lives once in `0.9-glossary.md`. Compliance mapping lives once in `0.13-compliance-mapping.md`. Other docs link.
- **Separation:** `0.0-prerequisites.md` is the operational checklist. `0.7-foundational-roadmap.md` is The Foundation, the story and narrative. They are separate files.
- **Audit Report name:** Security and Privacy Audit Report, or Audit Report, with no number in the name.
- **Phase vs Wave vs Section:** Phase = 0 to 10 roadmap block, 11 Phases. Wave = 1 to 5 delivery increment, 5 Waves. Section = single build unit inside a Phase, 56 Sections in the Roadmap.
- **Naming:** `phase-1` format throughout. `terraform/phases/phase-1/`, `docs/phases/phase-1-...`, `evidence/sections-after/phase-1/`.
- **Numbering:** All doc filenames use the numbering system in Sec 13.1. Phase 0 docs use `0.x`. Phase guides, evidence, and Terraform use `phase-N`.
- **Email separator:** Em-dash style. `Personal Gmail: funmi.personal@gmail.com — Funmi Daniels (Sales, AE)` and `WorkMail: funmi.daniels@aftercast.ai — Funmi Daniels (Sales, AE)`.
- **Fictional Gmail disclaimer:** The disclaimer appears on: cover, note after title, full disclaimer in Sec 2.2, footnote in Sec 4 / Table 3, footer, README, Appendix B.
- **Terminology:** DataClass tags are Restricted-PII and Restricted-PII + Sensitive. SPII is not a DataClass tag. Node (7-Node model) is used consistently. Transit A-F is used. Lifecycle Phases are: Collection, Ingestion, Distribution, Access, Replication, Retention.
- **After Architecture / Before Architecture:** Doc sections, titles, and table columns use After Architecture / Before Architecture. File names, in-AWS custom configs, resources, tags, and service names keep bare `after` / `before`.
- **RetentionDays:** Only 90, 180, 365, 1825. Pseudonymisation is a technique.
- **Counts:** The count is 143 total = 11 hired + 132 rejected.
