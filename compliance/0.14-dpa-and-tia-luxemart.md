## 4. DPA — Data Processing Agreement, GDPR Art 28, NDPA Sec 29

### 4.1 Subject Matter, Duration, Nature, Purpose

| Clause | Term |
| :--- | :--- |
| Subject matter | Processing of LuxeMart UK customer personal data by AfterCast AI, for forecasting. |

### 4.2 Instructions, GDPR Art 28(3)(a), NDPA Sec 29

- AfterCast AI processes personal data only on documented instructions from LuxeMart, including instructions in this DPA, in the service agreement, and in written instructions from LuxeMart.

- AfterCast AI processes for no own purposes, for no other clients, for no advertising, and for no purpose outside forecasting as instructed by LuxeMart.
- AfterCast AI does not sell LuxeMart personal data, does not share LuxeMart personal data with other clients, and does not use LuxeMart personal data to train models for other clients.

### 4.3 Confidentiality, GDPR Art 28(3)(b)

- Persons authorised to process personal data are bound by confidentiality, via employment contract, contractor agreement, or statutory duty.
- Access is by DataClass + Group, not by person. Sales-group only for Flow 1, via Lake Formation. Data Science group for training only, on hashed ID only. DPO + sales-group for mapping table. No Finance, no HR, no 30 staff, no ex-staff.

### 7.5 Proportionality — Access by Public Authorities

- Access by public authorities is assessed for: Lawfulness, necessity, proportionality, judicial oversight, transparency, and redress.
- Access is limited to what is necessary and proportionate in a democratic society, per GDPR Art 46 + SCCs, and NDPA Sec 41.
- Bulk, indiscriminate access, without judicial oversight, is not proportionate, and is not assumed to occur.
- Where AfterCast AI receives a request from a public authority for LuxeMart personal data, AfterCast AI informs LuxeMart, where law allows, and challenges the request where it is disproportionate, where law allows.
- Where AfterCast AI is prohibited by law from informing LuxeMart, AfterCast AI records the request, and informs LuxeMart when the prohibition lifts, where law allows.
- No request from a public authority for LuxeMart personal data has been received as of 18th Oct 2026, unless DPO records one in writing after this TIA.
