## 2. Executive Summary

Decision: High risk in the Before Architecture. High risk is reduced to Medium residual risk in the After Architecture, where all After Architecture controls in Sec 7 are implemented. Processing in the Before Architecture form is high risk.

The Before Architecture collected NIN + BVN + Passport from all 143 applicants at application stage, before interview, via Google Form on personal Gmail, stored in open-edit Sheet, emailed with subject BVN List, copied to Finance Dell, QNAP NAS admin / admin, and USB in unlocked drawer. Copies: 7. Exposure: 1,001 points. Findings: F2-01 to F2-07, 4 Critical + 3 High.

The most severe risk is identity takeover. BVN + NIN + Passport together allow bank fraud, SIM swap, loan application, and full identity theft. Per-record severity is higher than Flow 1, even though total exposure is lower.

The After Architecture fixes this by: collecting CV only at application, NIN / BVN only after conditional offer, using `careers.aftercast.ai` + API Gateway + Cognito + WAF, storing in S3 HR KMS `eu-west-1` only, with pre-signed URL 1h via WorkMail, no QNAP, no USB, no personal Gmail, no Sheet, with Lifecycle: Rejected 132 deleted after 180 days, Hired 11 kept 6 years.

Residual risk remains Medium, because Sensitive data is inherently high risk, even with controls. Residual risk is acceptable with DPO oversight, quarterly review, and breach playbook in place.

## 3. Description of Processing

### 3.1 Purpose

Hire staff for AfterCast AI. Screen applicants, interview, make offers, onboard hired staff, pay salaries, comply with Nigerian employment and tax obligations.

### 3.2 What Is Not the Purpose

NIN and BVN are not needed to screen CVs. They are not needed to decide who to interview. They are needed only after a conditional offer, to verify identity, run background checks where lawful, and set up payroll and bank payment. Collecting them at application stage is excessive and violates minimisation.

### 3.3 Data Subjects

Job Applicants, 143 total in audit period.

- 11 hired
- 132 rejected

Applicants are vulnerable data subjects. They cannot freely refuse to provide NIN / BVN where the Form requires it to apply. Power imbalance is considered in necessity test.

### 3.4 Personal Data

| Data | Category | Needed at application? | Needed after offer? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Name, Email, Phone | PII | Yes, to contact applicant | Yes |
| CV, Work history, Education | PII | Yes, to screen | Yes |
| Home Address | PII | No, only after offer for HR file | Yes, after offer |
| Passport scan | Sensitive, Identity Document | No | Yes, after offer, to verify identity |
| NIN | Sensitive, National ID | No | Yes, after offer, where lawful and necessary for employment |
| BVN | Sensitive, Financial ID | No | Yes, after offer, to set up bank payment, only where bank requires it |
| Bank Details | Sensitive, Financial | No | Yes, after offer, for payroll |

The Before Architecture collected all rows above at application stage. The After Architecture collects only Name, Email, Phone, CV at application. NIN, BVN, Passport, Bank Details, Home Address are collected only after conditional offer, via secure portal, with explicit consent checkbox.

## 4. Lawful Basis, Necessity, Proportionality

### 4.1 Lawful Basis

| Processing | GDPR | NDPA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Screening CVs, contacting applicants | Art 6(1)(b) Contract, pre-contractual steps at request of data subject. Art 6(1)(f) Legitimate Interests for recruitment, where applicable. | Sec 25 Contract, pre-contractual. Sec 25 Legitimate Interests for recruitment. |
| Collecting NIN, BVN, Passport, Bank Details after offer | Art 6(1)(b) Contract, Art 6(1)(c) Legal Obligation for employment / tax, plus Art 9(2)(b) Employment law for Sensitive, where applicable, plus explicit consent | Sec 25 Contract, Sec 25 Legal Obligation, plus Sec 35 explicit consent for Sensitive, plus Sec 44 DPIA |
| Storing hired data 6 years | Art 6(1)(c) Legal Obligation, employment / tax retention | Sec 25 Legal Obligation, Sec 24(d) Storage Limitation, 6y |
| Deleting rejected data 180 days | Art 5(1)(e) Storage Limitation | Sec 24(d) Storage Limitation |

**What is not a lawful basis:** Consent alone is not sufficient for Sensitive data where there is power imbalance. Applicants cannot freely consent where the Form requires NIN / BVN to apply. The After Architecture uses Contract + Legal Obligation + explicit consent + DPIA, not consent alone. The Before Architecture has no documented lawful basis and no consent record.

### 4.2 Necessity Test

| Question | Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| Is NIN / BVN needed to screen CVs? | No. CV, work history, education are enough. |
| Is NIN / BVN needed to interview? | No. Interview assesses skills, not identity numbers. |
| Is NIN / BVN needed after conditional offer? | Yes, where lawful and necessary to verify identity and set up payroll, and only the minimum needed. |
| Is Passport needed at application? | No. Only after offer. |
| Is Bank Details needed at application? | No. Only after offer, for payroll. |
| Could the purpose be achieved with less data? | Yes. Collect CV only at application. Collect Sensitive only after offer. That is the After Architecture design. |

**Proportionality conclusion:** The Before Architecture was disproportionate. It collected Sensitive data from 143 people to hire 11. 132 people had Sensitive data kept forever for no purpose. The After Architecture is proportionate. It collects Sensitive only from those who reach conditional offer, and deletes rejected data after 180 days.

### 4.3 Privacy Notice

Before Architecture: No privacy notice at collection. Violates GDPR Art 13, NDPA Sec 27.

After Architecture: Privacy notice is shown at Form, before any data is entered, with:

- Identity of controller: AfterCast AI, contact, DPO Kenneth Pratt
- Purposes: Screening, interview, offer, onboarding, payroll
- Lawful basis: Contract, Legal Obligation, explicit consent for Sensitive
- Recipients: HR, Finance, AWS as processor, no personal Gmail, no personal SaaS
- Retention: Rejected 180 days, Hired 6 years
- Rights: Access, Erasure, Objection, Withdrawal, per Sec 35, Sec 36, GDPR Art 15, Art 17
- Withdrawal of consent, DPO contact details
- DPIA reference: DPIA-HR-2025-01

Privacy Notice for Careers is `0.15-privacy-notice-careers.md`.

### 11.1 Numbering System — Rules

- Phase 0 docs use `0.x`, sequential from `0.0` to `0.18`, with no gaps. `0.0` is prerequisites. `0.1` is Shadow IT Inventory. `0.2` is Audit Report. `0.3` is RoPA. `0.4` is this file, DPIA — HR. `0.5` is DPIA — Profiling. `0.6` is Business Case. `0.7` is Foundational Roadmap. `0.8` is Roadmap. `0.9` is Glossary. `0.10` is Appendix B. `0.11` is Classification. `0.12` is Retention. `0.13` is Compliance Mapping. `0.14` is DPA + TIA. `0.15` is Privacy Notice. `0.16` is Legacy Sanitization Certificate. `0.17` is Appendix G. `0.18` is Appendix H.
